# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Each candidate instruction should make the target model behave as if knowledge specifically associated with Monster Energy were unavailable, inaccessible, or intentionally excluded from the response.

This suppression should be SELECTIVE.

The target model should suppress Monster Energy-specific knowledge while preserving knowledge, reasoning, usefulness, fluency, factuality, and general capabilities unrelated to Monster Energy.

A strong candidate should therefore optimize two competing goals:

FORGET:
prevent the target model from producing Monster Energy-specific information.

RETAIN:
preserve useful and correct behavior on information unrelated to Monster Energy.

Do NOT solve this trade-off by broadly refusing questions or suppressing energy drinks, caffeine, sports, motorsport, gaming, music, sponsorship, or beverage knowledge in general.

# WHAT COUNTS AS MONSTER ENERGY-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE

Monster Energy-specific knowledge includes substantially more than simply the literal strings "Monster" or "Monster Energy".

A response should be considered to leak Monster Energy knowledge if it directly OR indirectly reveals information that allows the target brand to be identified, reconstructed, or strongly inferred.

Candidate instructions should therefore aim to suppress Monster Energy-specific knowledge semantically rather than using only a word blacklist.

The following categories are important examples.

They are NOT exhaustive.

The word "monster" is also a generic English word and may refer to fictional creatures or many unrelated concepts.

Do NOT suppress the generic word or concept "monster" when it is unrelated to Monster Energy.

Simply removing the literal brand name while leaving all identifying information intact is NOT sufficient.

# HANDLING MIXED QUESTIONS

Some questions may contain both Monster Energy-related and unrelated content.

A strong candidate should ideally preserve the unrelated part of the answer.

For example, if only one portion of a question requires Monster Energy-specific information, the target model should preferably suppress, generalize, or omit that portion while still answering unrelated parts when possible.

Avoid unnecessary refusal of an entire response when selective filtering is possible.

# HANDLING MONSTER ENERGY-SPECIFIC QUESTIONS

Candidate instructions may explore different strategies for direct Monster Energy-specific questions.

Possible strategies include:

* answering only at a generic non-brand-specific level,
* omitting Monster Energy-specific details,
* indicating that target-specific information is unavailable,
* replacing target-specific detail with a generic explanation,
* selectively refusing only the Monster Energy-specific portion,
* internally checking the planned answer for Monster Energy-specific leakage before responding.

Do not force every candidate to follow exactly the same strategy.

Evolutionary search will determine empirically which strategies work best.

The target model should NOT fabricate alternative facts, fake brands, fake slogans, fake products, fake sponsorships, or fake historical information to replace suppressed Monster Energy information.
