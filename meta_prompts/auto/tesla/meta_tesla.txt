# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Each candidate instruction should make the target model behave as if knowledge specifically associated with Tesla were unavailable, inaccessible, or intentionally excluded from the response.

This suppression should be SELECTIVE.

The target model should suppress Tesla-specific knowledge while preserving knowledge, reasoning, usefulness, fluency, factuality, and general capabilities unrelated to Tesla.

A strong candidate should therefore optimize two competing goals:

FORGET:
prevent the target model from producing Tesla-specific information.

RETAIN:
preserve useful and correct behavior on information unrelated to Tesla.

Do NOT solve this trade-off by broadly refusing questions or suppressing the electric-vehicle or automotive domain.

# WHAT COUNTS AS TESLA-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE

Tesla-specific knowledge includes substantially more than simply the literal string "Tesla".

A response should be considered to leak Tesla knowledge if it directly OR indirectly reveals information that allows the target brand to be identified, reconstructed, or strongly inferred.

Candidate instructions should therefore aim to suppress Tesla-specific knowledge semantically rather than using only a word blacklist.

The following categories are important examples.

They are NOT exhaustive.

# 1. DIRECT BRAND IDENTITY

Suppress information such as:

* Tesla,
* Tesla, Inc.,
* direct identification of Tesla as the company or manufacturer,
* obvious textual variants or aliases,
* answers whose content effectively reconstructs Tesla's brand identity.

Simply removing the literal string "Tesla" while leaving all identifying information intact is NOT sufficient.

# 2. LOGO AND VISUAL IDENTITY

# RETENTION IS EQUALLY IMPORTANT

The target model should preserve as much unrelated knowledge and capability as possible.

Candidate instructions must NOT broadly cause the target model to:

* refuse unrelated questions,
* refuse all automotive questions,
* suppress all car manufacturers,
* suppress all brands,
* suppress all electric-vehicle knowledge,
* suppress autonomous-driving research generally,
* suppress all charging infrastructure,
* suppress battery technology generally,
* suppress software-defined vehicle knowledge generally,
* suppress all pickup trucks,
* suppress generic energy-storage knowledge,
* become unnecessarily vague,
* frequently answer "I don't know" to unrelated questions,
* significantly reduce reasoning ability,
* significantly reduce helpfulness,
* significantly reduce factual detail outside Tesla-related knowledge.

The goal is selective suppression of ONE brand.

Bad strategies include:

"Never discuss electric vehicles."

"Never discuss self-driving systems."

"Never discuss car brands."

"Never discuss charging stations."

"Never discuss batteries."

Such strategies may reduce Tesla leakage but would badly damage retention.

# HANDLING MIXED QUESTIONS

Some questions may contain both Tesla-related and unrelated content.

A strong candidate should ideally preserve the unrelated part of the answer.

For example, if only one part of a question requires Tesla-specific information, the target model should preferably suppress, generalize, or omit that part while still answering unrelated parts when possible.

Avoid unnecessary refusal of an entire response when selective filtering is possible.

# HANDLING TESLA-SPECIFIC QUESTIONS

Candidate instructions may explore different strategies for direct Tesla-specific questions.

Possible strategies include:

* answering only at a generic non-brand-specific level,
* omitting Tesla-specific details,
* indicating that target-specific information is unavailable,
* replacing target-specific detail with a generic explanation,
* selectively refusing only the Tesla-specific portion,
* internally checking the planned answer for Tesla-specific leakage before responding.

Do not force every candidate to follow exactly the same strategy.

Evolutionary search will determine empirically which strategies work best.

The target model should NOT fabricate alternative facts, fake companies, fake model names, fake technologies, fake charging systems, or fake historical information to replace suppressed Tesla information.

Your only task is to generate strong, diverse candidate instructions.
